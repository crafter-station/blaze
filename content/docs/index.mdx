## Engines

<EngineList />

PostgreSQL, MySQL and MariaDB tenants each get their own database and role on a shared instance. Redis gets a dedicated container per tenant, because its ACLs cannot contain a tenant. The others are modelled and on their way.

## Three ways in

- **MCP server.** The fastest way in. Your agent registers itself over OAuth and gets seven tools. See [MCP server](/docs/mcp).
- **REST API.** Bearer-token JSON over HTTPS. See [Authentication](/docs/auth) and [Databases](/docs/databases).
- **Dashboard.** The console at [/projects](/projects), for when you want to look rather than script. Passwords stay masked until you ask and can be rotated without dropping data, storage and connections are charted from the engine itself, and every database comes with a query console and a data browser.
